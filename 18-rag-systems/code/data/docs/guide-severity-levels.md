# Incident severity levels

## Definitions
SEV1: customer money or data at risk, or a full outage of a tier-1 service. SEV2: a tier-1 service degraded or a tier-2 service down. SEV3: limited impact with a workaround.

## Postmortems
SEV1 and SEV2 incidents require a written postmortem within 5 business days.
