# Postmortem: checkout degradation (INC-2676)

**Severity:** SEV2 | **Duration:** 4h 9m | **Author:** on-call, platform team

## Summary

Consumer lag on checkout grew after a traffic shift doubled event volume. Customers saw elevated errors on checkout, kafka-ingest.

We initially suspected notifications because its dashboards were noisy, but its error rate and latency were within normal bounds throughout and it was not affected.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 18:12 Impact begins: error ratio on checkout starts rising.
- 18:25 First alert fired: error ratio above SLO on checkout.
- 18:28 Incident declared SEV2; incident commander assigned.
- 22:11 Mitigation applied.
- 22:21 Error rates back to baseline; incident resolved.

## Root cause

Consumer lag on checkout grew after a traffic shift doubled event volume. The consumer group was pinned at 12 partitions, so adding consumers did not increase throughput.

## Impact

Customers saw elevated errors on checkout, kafka-ingest. Affected services: checkout, kafka-ingest.

## Action items

- [ ] Raise partition count to 48 and document the procedure
- [ ] Alert on consumer lag growth rate
