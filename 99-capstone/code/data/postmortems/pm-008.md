# Postmortem: ledger degradation (INC-2544)

**Severity:** SEV1 | **Author:** on-call, core team

## Summary

Consumer lag on ledger grew after a traffic shift doubled event volume. Customers saw elevated errors on ledger, notifications.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 17:40 First alert fired: error ratio above SLO on ledger.
- 17:43 Incident declared SEV1; incident commander assigned.
- 17:45 Mitigation applied.
- 17:51 Error rates back to baseline; incident resolved.

Later log analysis showed impact actually began at 17:12, before any alert.

Customer-visible impact lasted from 17:12 until resolution at 17:51.

## Root cause

Consumer lag on ledger grew after a traffic shift doubled event volume. The consumer group was pinned at 12 partitions, so adding consumers did not increase throughput.

## Impact

Customers saw elevated errors on ledger, notifications. Affected services: ledger, notifications.

## Action items

- [ ] Raise partition count to 48 and document the procedure
- [ ] Alert on consumer lag growth rate
