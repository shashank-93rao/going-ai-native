# Postmortem: ledger degradation (INC-2573)

**Severity:** SEV2 | **Duration:** 2h 51m | **Author:** on-call, core team

## Summary

Consumer lag on ledger grew after a traffic shift doubled event volume. Customers saw elevated errors on ledger, payments, search.

## Timeline (UTC)

- 09:15 First alert fired: error ratio above SLO on ledger.
- 09:18 Incident declared SEV2; incident commander assigned.
- 11:35 Mitigation applied.
- 11:46 Error rates back to baseline; incident resolved.

Later log analysis showed impact actually began at 08:55, before any alert.

## Root cause

Consumer lag on ledger grew after a traffic shift doubled event volume. The consumer group was pinned at 12 partitions, so adding consumers did not increase throughput.

## Impact

Customers saw elevated errors on ledger, payments, search. Affected services: ledger, payments, search.

## Action items

- [ ] Raise partition count to 48 and document the procedure
- [ ] Alert on consumer lag growth rate
